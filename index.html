RakshaPay: stops UPI scams before you pay

Self-initiated and led end to end. It screens payment SMS, QR codes and links before a transaction is authorised, and every model runs on the phone, so no message data leaves the device.

What I built

Started from research on real Indian UPI scam patterns: fake collect requests, tampered QR payees, lookalike payment links and SMS-led social engineering. Then collected data, trained three models in Python, and shipped an Android app plus a Next.js web suite that runs the same models in the browser, backed by Supabase.

119 automated tests pin the mobile and web apps to one parity fixture.

OilShield AI

A safety triage platform built for Oil India Limited's HSSE department. It reads field incident reports written in English, Hindi, Hinglish or Assamese terms, and flags the ones that could lead to serious injury or fatality.

Two ML models, one for serious-injury precursors and one for the 10 IOGP Life-Saving Rules, trained on 500 safety reports across 12+ installations. It also finds similar past near-misses, draws a safety knowledge graph, forecasts risk, and has four role-based views, human review and CI tests.

Deja Vu

A memory layer for bugs you have already solved. Built for DevCrest Buildathon '26 (Mistake-Memo AI), it matches problems by their underlying root cause instead of their wording, so a Python async bug can recall a similar JavaScript one.

In its own test, the right match scored 0.587 and a closer-worded decoy scored 0.349. Memories fade over time, merge into patterns, and the nudge appears while you type. It also has a 3D memory map.
