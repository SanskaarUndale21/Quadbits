Annora: Autonomous Kitchen System

A nationally funded project (₹4.5 Lakh NAIN 2.0 grant): a fully autonomous cooking system with Raspberry Pi 4B and ESP32 controlling hardware, paired with a companion app for recipe management and nutritional tracking.

On-device payment security platform engineered to analyze scam vectors, fake collect requests, lookalike links, and SMS social engineering with zero message leakage from the device.
